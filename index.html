What are art and hobbies?

Art is a way to show ideas, feelings and creativity. A hobby is an activity that people enjoy in their free time. Art can become a hobby and help people learn new skills.

Painting

Painting is the process of making pictures with colours.

You can use watercolours, acrylic paints or pencils. Painting develops imagination and creativity.

📷

Photography

Photography helps us capture interesting moments.

A good photo can tell a story. You can start with a phone camera and practise composition.

🎸

Music

Music includes singing and playing instruments.

Learning an instrument takes practice. It can also improve concentration and listening skills.

🧶

Knitting

Knitting is a creative hobby that uses yarn and needles.

People make scarves, hats and other useful things. It is a good way to practise patience.

📚

Reading

Reading lets us explore new stories and ideas.

Books can teach us about different people, places and cultures.

🗿

Sculpture

Sculpture is the art of making three-dimensional objects.

Artists can work with clay, wood, stone or other materials.

Why are hobbies important?

  • They help us relax after studying or working.
  • They develop creativity and imagination.
  • They help us learn useful skills.
  • They give us a chance to meet people with similar interests.
